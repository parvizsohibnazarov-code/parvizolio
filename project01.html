Project 1: Arc Reactor

The Arc Reactor → Iron Man → Tony Stark. Genius, Playboy, Philanthropist, but most importantly, one of the greatest engineers in the Marvel Universe. He played a huge role in my decision to become an engineer, and I do aspire to be somewhat like him. The Arc Reactor is probably the coolest fictional invention ever, and it resonates with me because it forces me to think of unconventional ways to solve problems (after all Tony Stark built it in a cave).

I enjoyed building this project (I even selected the “Tony Stark’s Workshop Playlist” while building it), although I did not have some parts that would have enhanced the appearance. But, just like Stark building his reactor in a cave with limited resources, I decided to use my roommate’s desk lamp to simulate the glowing component of the reactor. I think this project was a symbolic start to my engineering career.
