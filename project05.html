Project 5: Spinograph

Executive Summary

This project involved designing and programming an automated drawing machine (Spirograph) capable of generating complex geometric patterns. Built using the LEGO SPIKE Prime kit and programmed in MicroPython, the machine utilizes two independently controlled motors: a Large Motor that rotates the paper platen and a Medium Motor that drives the pen mechanism. By adjusting the relative angular velocity between the two axes in code, the system produces precise, repeating mathematical curves.

Key Technical Features & Engineering Principles

1. Dual-Axis Independent Kinematics

  • Paper Base (Axis A): Driven by the Large Motor to rotate the paper platform steadily.
  • Pen Mechanism (Axis B): Driven by a Medium Motor to create repeating lateral arc sweeps across the paper surface.
  • Parametric Curve Generation: The resulting drawn pattern is a continuous mathematical curve determined by the frequency ratio, phase, and relative speeds of the two motors.

2. Algorithmic Ratio Optimization (MicroPython)

  • Speed Ratio (5:7): Through testing different speed combinations, a 5:7 motor speed ratio (Large Motor to Medium Motor) was selected as the optimal configuration.
  • Symmetry & Closure: A 5:7 ratio ensures that the pen path creates a balanced, multi-lobed geometric pattern that closes perfectly without prematurely drawing over previous lines.
  • Execution Time: Optimized the code execution loop to complete a full, high-density pattern cycle in 30 seconds.

Design Iteration Process

  1. Iteration 1: Gear Slap & Structural Rigidity
    • Problem: Early physical builds had too much gear backlash (play), causing the pen to jump and produce jagged, asymmetrical lines.
    • Solution: Stiffened the pen-arm frame and braced the turntable platform to eliminate mechanical play and vibration.
  2. Iteration 2: Pen Pressure Calibration
    • Problem: Too much downward pen pressure stalled the turntable motor, while too little pressure caused faint, broken lines.
    • Solution: Engineered a counterweighted, passive pivot for the pen arm, allowing gravity to provide consistent, light contact against the rotating paper.
  3. Iteration 3: Software Speed Ratio Testing (MicroPython)
    • Problem: A 1:1 speed ratio produced simple, boring circles/ellipses, while non-integer random ratios caused messy over-scribbling.
    • Solution: Systematically tested fractional ratios (1:2, 2:3, 3:5) before settling on the 5:7 ratio, which yielded the best balance of visual complexity, line density, and rapid 30-second completion.
